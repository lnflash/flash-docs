This page is a short overview of the Flash app for new users, with links to the full guides.

## What you can do in the app

- Start with a Trial account, then verify it and add capabilities in the *Do more with Flash* hub
- Use the **Cash Wallet** (custodial, held by Flash) for dollars
- Optionally enable the **Bitcoin Wallet** (non-custodial, backed up by your recovery phrase) in Advanced Mode
- **Swap** between Cash and Bitcoin once Advanced Mode is on
- **Transfer**: top up by card or bank, and settle to a bank account, once your account is verified
- Enable **Chat** to message other Flash users and the support group
- Find merchants and Flashpoints on the **Map**
- Add a **Flashcard** and see its balance

## Recommended onboarding order

1. **Get Started**: tap *Start* to create a Trial account, or *Login* to restore one with your phone number or email
2. **Upgrade Your Account**: *Verify your account*, then add *Bank cash-out*, a *USD Virtual Bank Account*, or *Business* as you need them
3. **Cash Wallet**: understand your dollar balance
4. **Bitcoin Wallet**: enable Advanced Mode only if you want to hold Bitcoin, and back it up straight away
5. **Swap**: move value between the two
6. **Transfer**: top up and settle between your bank and Flash

## Important note

The Cash Wallet and the Bitcoin Wallet are held differently: Flash holds the Cash balance and restores it when you log in; you hold the Bitcoin Wallet through its recovery phrase and Flash cannot recover it for you. Start with:
- [Managing Your Bitcoin](/manage-your-sats/)
- [Cash Wallet](/guides/cash-wallet/)
- [Bitcoin Wallet](/guides/bitcoin-wallet/)
- [Swap Between Wallets](/guides/swap/)
