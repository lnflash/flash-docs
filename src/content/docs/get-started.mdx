Lightning-fast Bitcoin payments in three simple steps.

<Steps>

1. **Download Flash**

   Get the Flash app on your phone from the [App Store or Google Play](https://flashapp.me/app).

2. **Create your wallet**

   Open the app and tap **Start** — no signup required. Setup is instant, no personal
   information is needed, and your keys stay yours.

3. **Add funds**

   Get cash into your wallet and start sending payments. See
   [How to Add Funds](/guides/get-cash/).

</Steps>
