Lightning-fast Bitcoin payments in three simple steps.

<Steps>

1. **Download Flash**

   Get the Flash app on your phone from the [App Store or Google Play](https://getflash.io/app).

2. **Create your wallet**

   Open the app and tap **Start**. That creates a Trial account on this phone in seconds,
   with no form to fill in. Flash holds this Cash balance for you, so verify the account
   with your phone number or email soon after, so you can log back in on any phone.

3. **Add funds**

   Get cash into your wallet and start sending payments. See
   [How to Add Funds](/guides/get-cash/).

</Steps>

## What you can do with Flash
