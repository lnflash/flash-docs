---
title: How to Top Up
description: Add money to your Cash Wallet with a debit or credit card, a Jamaican bank transfer, or a US bank transfer where the international option is enabled.
---

Top Up lives under **Transfer** on the home screen. It adds money to your Cash Wallet (the app calls the balance *Cash* or your *USD wallet*).

:::note
**Transfer** appears once your account is verified. Trial accounts do not see it. See [How to Upgrade Your Account](/guides/upgrade-account/).
:::

---

## Choose a method

Tap **Transfer** → **Top Up**. Under *Top up via* you will see the methods available to your account:

| Method | Good for | Minimum |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Debit/Credit Card | Fast, self-service, card fees apply | US$10 by default (the app shows the current minimum) |
| Bank Transfer | Jamaican bank accounts, support-assisted | Set with support |
| International Bank Transfer | A US account and routing number in your name, where enabled | US$20 per ACH deposit |

---

## Debit/Credit Card

1. Tap **Debit/Credit Card** and enter the amount. The screen shows what you will receive "after card and processing fees" and notes that "Card payments top up your USD wallet".
2. Tap Continue. A secure card checkout page opens inside the app.
3. Pay. The credit lands in your Cash Wallet once the card payment is confirmed.

Card top-ups have a daily limit; the app tells you if an amount "exceeds your remaining daily limit". They are not available on Trial accounts ("Card top-ups are not available for your account level").

---

## Bank Transfer (Jamaica)

1. Tap **Bank Transfer**.
2. The app asks you to contact support for the banking details and offers a **Chat on WhatsApp** button. Support gives you the account to pay and the reference to use.
3. Make the transfer from your bank. The credit appears in your Cash Wallet after Flash receives it, typically the same business day for transfers made during business hours.

Flash applies a 2% fee to bank-transfer top-ups.

Flash's bank details are not published here. Get them from support each time, and do not rely on details from an old message.

---

## International Bank Transfer (US bank account)

Where this option is enabled, Flash gives you a **USD Virtual Bank Account**: a US account and routing number in your name that can receive ACH transfers, wires, and payroll.

1. Tap **International Bank Transfer**. The first time, complete the identity check (Bridge). While it is pending, the row shows *pending*.
2. Once approved, the app shows your virtual account's bank name, account number, and routing number.
3. Send from any US bank to those details. The minimum is US$20 for ACH. The credit appears in your Cash Wallet after the transfer settles.

---

## In person

Some Flashpoints exchange cash. On the [Map](/guides/map/), a merchant card with the **Cash** badge offers it. Pay the merchant in cash and they send the amount to your Flash username. Agree the amount and any charge with the merchant before you hand over cash.
