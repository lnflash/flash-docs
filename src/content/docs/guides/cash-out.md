title: How to Cash Out
description: Settle your Cash Wallet balance to a Jamaican bank account, or to a US bank account where the international option is enabled, from the Transfer menu in the Flash app.
---

Cashing out is called **Settle** in the app. It moves money from your Cash Wallet to a bank account, and it is self-service: you do not send funds to a Flash username, and you do not need to message support to start it.

---

## Before You Begin

- **Verify your account and set up Bank cash-out.** Tap **Upgrade your account** on the home screen (or Settings → Account) to open the *Do more with Flash* hub. Complete **Verify your account**, then **Bank cash-out**, where you upload an ID and add your Jamaican bank details. The request goes for review: "Your bank cash-out request has been submitted for review." See [How to Upgrade Your Account](/guides/upgrade-account/).
- **Trial accounts do not see Transfer.** A verified account that has not set up Bank cash-out sees *Account upgrade required* when it taps Settle.
- **Have a bank account on file.** Without one the app stops with "No bank account found. Please add a bank account first." Your accounts are under Settings → **Bank accounts**.

---

## Settle to a Jamaican bank account (JMD)

1. On the home screen tap **Transfer**, then **Settle**.
2. Under *Settle to*, choose **JMD Bank Account**.
3. Enter the amount, or use the percentage buttons (*% to settle*). The screen shows the settlement rate ("Settlement rate: US$1 = J$…") and an estimate: "You'll receive about … in your bank account. Final amount is confirmed on the next screen."
4. Tap **Next** and review the offer: **Exchange Rate**, **Send Amount**, **Receive Amount**, **Fee**, and **Withdraw to** (the bank account). The offer is valid for a short time ("Valid for …"). If it expires, go back and request it again.
5. Confirm. The app reports "Settlement request initiated successfully."

The rate and the fee are part of the offer you review in step 4. They are set by Flash at that moment and shown as amounts; this page does not list a fixed percentage because the app does not.

**Timing.** The app's own notice: bank transfers are usually confirmed the same day, but may take longer if submitted on weekdays after 2:00 pm or on Fridays and weekends. Transactions after 2:00 pm on a weekday are not confirmed by the bank until the next business day. Contact support if you do not see the funds within 2–3 business days.

## Settle to an international bank account (USD)

Where the international option is enabled for your account, *Settle to* also lists **International Bank Account**.

1. The first time, the app runs an identity check (Bridge). While it is pending, the row shows *pending*; once approved, continue.
2. Link a US bank account: connect it through Plaid, or enter it manually (bank name, account owner, 9-digit routing number, account number, checking or savings, and your US address). Linked accounts appear under Settings → **Bank accounts**.
3. Enter the amount and review. A Flash service fee of 0.5% is included in the withdrawal amount and shown on the review screen.

## Fees

| Destination | Fee |
|-------------|-----|
| JMD Bank Account | Shown as an amount in the settlement offer you review before confirming |
| International Bank Account | 0.5% Flash service fee, shown on the review screen |

Earlier versions of this page listed fixed 2% and 1% tiers and a same-day RTGS cut-off. Neither is what the app shows today.
