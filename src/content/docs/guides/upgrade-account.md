title: How to Upgrade Your Account
description: Verify your Flash account and add capabilities like bank cash-out, a USD virtual bank account, and a business profile from the Do more with Flash hub.
---

Flash accounts start as **Trial** accounts. Verifying makes you **Verified**; adding a business profile makes you **Business**. Each extra capability is a separate setup in one hub, so you add only what you need.

| Status | What you can do |
|--------|-----------------|
| Trial account | Send and receive, with reduced transaction limits and no recovery method: if you lose the phone or uninstall the app before verifying, the balance is lost |
| Verified | Log back in with your phone number or email; higher limits; **Transfer** appears on Home |
| Verified + Bank cash-out | Settle to a Jamaican bank account (badge: *Bank payout*) |
| Verified + USD Virtual Bank Account | A US account and routing number for ACH, wires, and payroll (badge: *USD account*) |
| Business | Business profile for a Flashpoint, plus everything above |

---

## Where to go

- Home: the **Upgrade your account** card
- Settings → **Account** → **Upgrade your account** (Trial), or **Request Upgrade** / **Edit Upgrade Request** later

Both open **Do more with Flash**, which shows your status and the rows below.

---

## Verify your account

**What you need:** your phone number. Email is optional.

1. Tap **Verify your account**.
2. Enter your full name and phone number (email optional).
3. Choose **Send via SMS** or **Send via WhatsApp** and enter the 6-digit code; it submits itself when complete.
4. The app confirms "Your account is verified."

---

## Bank cash-out

**What you need:** a government-issued ID and, to settle later, your Jamaican bank details.

1. Under *Ways to get paid*, tap **Bank cash-out**.
2. Personal information, then optional business information; tick **Do you want a Flash terminal?** if you want a POS device.
3. Banking information: upload your ID (required) and add your bank name, branch, account type, currency, and account number. Enter the account number exactly as your bank shows it.
4. Tap **Next** to submit. The app confirms "Your bank cash-out request has been submitted for review."

While the request is under review the home card reads *Upgrade request is pending*, and Settings → Account offers **Edit Upgrade Request** if you need to change something. When it is approved, the *Bank payout* badge appears and **Transfer → Settle** works. See [How to Cash Out](/guides/cash-out/).

Bank details can be added or changed later under Settings → **Bank accounts**. Settle needs at least one on file.

---

## USD Virtual Bank Account

Available after verification, where the international option is enabled. Tap **USD Virtual Bank Account**, complete the identity check (Bridge), and the app issues a US account and routing number in your name. Deposits arrive in your Cash Wallet; the same check also enables **International Bank Account** under Settle. The row shows *In review* while the check is pending. See [How to Top Up](/guides/top-up/#international-bank-transfer-us-bank-account).

---

## Business

**What you need:** your business name and address, an ID, and full bank details.

1. Under *Grow*, tap **Business**.
2. Enter the business name and pick the address from the map search.
3. Upload your ID and complete all bank fields (name, branch, account type, currency, account number).
4. Submit. The app confirms "Your business upgrade request has been submitted for review."

Approved businesses can be listed as a Flashpoint on the [Map](/guides/map/) and use [Flash POS](/training/flash-pos/). See [Flash Business Accounts](/business/).

---

## Limits

Settings → **Account** → **Transaction Limits** opens *Spending Limits*. Limits are set in US dollars and shown in your local currency at the current rate, so the local figure moves with the exchange rate. Card top-ups and ACH deposits have their own rows.

## Troubleshooting

**My request has been pending for a while.** Reviews are done by the Flash team; the app does not show a timeline. Contact support from Settings → **Need help? Contact us.** with your username.

**I need to change what I submitted.** Settings → Account → **Edit Upgrade Request** reopens the form with your answers filled in.

**Bank cash-out fails with "No bank account found".** The request went through without bank details. Add an account under Settings → **Bank accounts**.
