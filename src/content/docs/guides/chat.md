Flash Chat is a private messenger built into the app on the Nostr protocol. It is off until you turn it on.

---

## Turning Chat on

- Settings → **Chat Settings** → **Enable Chat**, or
- Settings → **Need help? Contact us.** → **App chat**, which enables it on the way to the support group

Once enabled, a **Chat** tab appears in the bottom bar next to Home, Map, and Scan. Your chat identity is a Nostr key pair created on your phone; Settings → Chat Settings shows your public profile ID and lets you export the private key. Keep the private key private: it is your chat identity.

---

## What you can do

- Message any Flash user by username, or any Nostr user by public key (npub)
- Swipe a message to send a quoted reply
- Long-press a message to react with an emoji
- Send sats to the person you are chatting with
- Join the pinned **Support Group Chat** and other groups
- Post and read a Nostr profile

---

## Starting a chat

1. Open the **Chat** tab. The top of the screen has three icon tabs: chats, profile, and contacts.
2. On the chats tab, use the search field ("username or nostr pubkey") to find someone. It searches the network, not just your contacts.
3. Tap the result, type your message, and send.

You can also open a chat from a contact's profile (the chat-bubble button) or from a merchant's map card (**Chat**, shown when the merchant has a username).

---

## Sending sats from a chat

The flash icon in the conversation header sends your default zap amount to the person you are chatting with. Long-press it to open **Send Zap** and choose or edit a preset (the defaults are 21, 100, 500, and 1000 sats). The payment goes through your Flash wallet like any other send.

---

## The support group

The **Support Group Chat** is pinned at the top of your chats. Use it to ask the Flash team about your account, a stuck payment, or a top-up or settlement. Do not paste a recovery phrase, login code, or bank details into any chat.

---

## Good to know

- Messages between Flash users are end-to-end encrypted (NIP-17 direct messages).
- Chat is for talking, not for moving money: use **Send** or the zap button to pay.
- Turning Chat off in Settings hides the tab; your key pair stays on the phone.
